# Demo script (about 8 minutes)

Open with the sentence: *"EcoMetrix helps a city planner decide where the next rupee of greening should go, and shows what it will achieve and cost, with honest ranges."*

1. **Atlas (1.5 min).** Scroll. Trees counted, where the ground is hottest, who can walk to a park. Say: every number wears a label (point to MEASURED / MODELLED).
2. **Data audit (45 s).** "Before building on a city, we check it." Show the pilot checks and any failed layer: honesty is the point.
3. **Forecast (1 min).** Chart, backtest table. "The method had to beat *no change* on years it never saw." Say the band is a backtest error envelope, not a statistical interval.
4. **Priority (1 min).** Presets change the weights; "weights are policy choices, not facts." Mention no-regret.
5. **Plan (2 min).** Pick a preset and budget; show outlined sites, people counted once, cost range, the do-nothing baseline, and the gain over funding highest-need first. Click a site: evidence card, "needs field verification".
6. **Compare (45 s).** Three plans and doing nothing; what differs.
7. **Citizen view (45 s).** Switch to मराठी, tap a place. Mention the review notice.
8. **Evaluation (45 s).** Sensitivity bars: "these are the assumptions to research first." Honest-claims table, and the human checks still to do.

**Close with limits:** simulated, not built; sources are ranges; sites are estimates of open land; surface not air temperature; no payback claimed.

**Backup:** the recorded video (record it after the final data build) and the printable report (`/report`).
**If the server is asleep:** say "free hosting wakes in about a minute", keep talking through the Atlas copy.
