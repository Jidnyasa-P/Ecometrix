# Usability test protocol (five participants)

Recruit 5 people: ideally 1 or 2 from planning or sustainability work, and 3 non-experts. Avoid the Week 1 interviewees. 20 to 30 minutes each, screen shared, think aloud. Do not help unless they are stuck for 2 minutes; note where.

## Tasks (read aloud, one at a time)
1. Find the hottest area that has the least access to a park. *(Priority screen)*
2. Set a budget and a priority, and read the recommended portfolio. *(Plan screen)*
3. Pick one site and explain in your own words why it was chosen. *(Evidence card)* **Key test.**
4. Say which numbers on the screen are measured and which are estimates. *(labels)*
5. Compare two plans and say what is different. *(Compare)*
6. (Non-experts) Open the citizen view and say what is planned near a place you know.

## Record per participant
Task success (yes / partly / no), time, errors and where they hesitated, and their own words for task 3.
After the tasks: the 10 System Usability Scale statements, each 1 to 5. SUS = ((sum of odd items − 5) + (25 − sum of even items)) × 2.5.
